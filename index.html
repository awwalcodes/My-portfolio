What I do?

I design intuitive, user-centered digital experiences with a strong understanding of how they’re built. My background in frontend development helps me design interfaces that are not only visually clean, but also practical, scalable, and easy for developers to implement.

I handle the full UX process—from research and wireframing to high-fidelity UI—while using my knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to make informed design decisions.
